Presentation tips

Best practices for a client-ready demonstration

1. Use your own domain.
Host this site under a clinic or agency domain and allow that origin in your public voice key settings.

2. Keep the existing workflow.
No need to recreate the booking agent or your Calendar scenario.

3. Demonstrate real bookings safely.
Use a demo calendar and test patient names. Show the actual Calendar after a confirmed booking.

4. Distinguish demo vs live data.
Dashboard sample statistics are previews, not analytics collected from the voice platform.

5. Protect access.
Add authentication and role-based access before exposing patient records or call recordings to a real clinic.
